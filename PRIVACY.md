# Shift Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Contact:** shiftbudgeting@gmail.com

---

### Privacy Statement & Data Protection
Your privacy and data security are paramount. All financial records, income streams, and budget entries are isolated within your private cloud workspace protected by Google Cloud Firestore security rules.

* **Zero Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, license, rent, or share user financial data with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing affiliates.
* **Account Deletion & Data Purge:** When you delete your account, all associated shifts, budgets, income logs, expenses, and personal profile documents are permanently purged immediately.
* **Inactivity & Data Retention:** Accounts with no user activity for twenty-four (24) consecutive months are scheduled for permanent deletion following 30-day and 7-day advance email warnings.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We only collect data necessary to provide and synchronize your budgeting workspaces:
* **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, and avatar URL provided via Google Authentication.
* **Financial Planning Data:** Income streams, monthly fixed and variable expenses, recurring subscriptions, and savings goal milestones entered into Shift.
* **Workspace Collaboration:** Collaborator email addresses and role assignments (Owner, Editor, Viewer).

---

### 2. How We Use Your Data
Your data is used strictly to calculate projected cash flow, joint allocations, surplus models, and savings forecasts. We do not analyze your financial figures for marketing, credit scoring, or advertising personalization.

---

### 3. Storage & Data Security
Shift leverages Google Cloud Firestore with multi-tenant database security rules. Data is encrypted in transit via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) and encrypted at rest on Google Cloud storage infrastructure.

---

### 4. Zero Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, license, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to data brokers, ad networks, or marketing partners. We do not employ third-party behavioral analytics or invasive tracking cookies.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Storage
* **Account Inactivity:** We retain your personal information, scenario models, and budget figures only for as long as your account remains active. If an account exhibits no login or user activity for twenty-four (24) consecutive months, the account and all associated documents will be scheduled for permanent deletion. We provide advance email notifications thirty (30) days and seven (7) days prior to deletion. Logging in resets the clock.
* **User-Initiated Deletion:** You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. When you select **Delete Account** in Shift Settings, all shifts, workspaces, budgets, income items, expenses, subscriptions, and profile credentials are permanently erased immediately from our databases.

---
